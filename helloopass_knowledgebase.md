## About HellOOpass

HellOOpass is one of the most advanced payment Operating Systems in the world, designed to empower communities in the simplest and most accessible way possible. It acts as the engine facilitating trading and payments inside a community — covering wallet creation, KYC verification, payment processing, merchant licensing, sponsor top-ups, and gig worker payouts — all in a seamless experience that works even on basic phones.

## What is a Community?

A HellOOpass community is a closed group that can include buyers, merchants, beneficiaries, sponsors, sales agents, and gig workers — all transacting using Stablepoints within a shared digital ecosystem. Points stay within the community and cannot be used outside of it.

Anyone in the community can acquire Stablepoints by topping up via mobile money, bank transfer, or community agents. Points can only be used to purchase goods and services from within the community.

When it comes to cashing out, two categories of members are allowed to convert points back to real cash:
- **Licensed merchants** — those holding a valid official merchant license in their country
- **Gig workers** — as service providers, they earn points by delivering services and can cash out their earnings

Regular end users, sponsors, and buyers cannot cash out directly.

---

## Stablepoints

Stablepoints work similarly to stablecoins in the crypto world — a digital currency always backed 1:1 by real cash. For every Stablepoint in circulation, an equivalent amount of real cash is held in the community's own bank account.

The community itself — not HellOOpass — is the custodian of the funds. HellOOpass simply provides the OS layer that makes it all work securely, transparently, and instantly.

**Example:** 1 Stablepoint = 1 USD, 1 EUR, or 100 CFA — depending on the community's currency.

## The 5 Community Models

### Model 1 — Cooperative / Union
*Member-funded*

Members of a union or cooperative fund their own association. Each member tops up their HellOOpass wallet with real cash, acquires Stablepoints, and buys from any member who holds a merchant license within the community. Merchants collect Stablepoints from sales and can transfer them back to real cash.

This is a fully peer-funded closed-loop economy — ideal for savings groups, cooperatives, and trade unions.

---

### Model 2 — Disbursement
*NGO / Government*

An NGO, government, or institution sponsors a community of beneficiaries such as women or youth. Beneficiaries receive a credit line they can use to purchase goods and services from merchants and service providers accepted into the community. Merchants can cash out their sales instantly.

This model enables targeted financial inclusion and social support programs at scale.

---

### Model 3 — Distribution
*Corporate network*

A corporation operates a closed network with one unique seller (the corporation itself), multiple sales agents managing points of sale, and multiple buyers. Buyers top up their HellOOpass account and purchase from the network, benefiting from payment facilities and loyalty programs.

Ideal for retail chains, distributors, or franchise networks looking to digitise payments and reward customers.

---

### Model 4 — Gift Card
*Diaspora / Sponsors*

Designed primarily for diaspora members who want to support family or friends while controlling how money is spent. Sponsors log into a dedicated web portal and generate funded wallets for their beneficiaries.

Beneficiaries can only spend points at pre-approved dedicated places. For example, a Health Gift Wallet restricts spending to pharmacies and clinics only — ensuring funds are used exactly as intended.

---

### Model 5 — E-commerce
*Online marketplace*

A standard online marketplace where suppliers register with a HellOOpass earnings wallet to receive payments. Buyers can either pay directly (credit/debit card) for goods they order, or pay using their regular HellOOpass wallet.

This model bridges traditional e-commerce with the HellOOpass payment ecosystem, giving suppliers access to both wallet users and standard online shoppers.

## KYC Verification

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is done only once — for your first community. You'll need to provide:
- Your full name (as shown on official ID)
- Date of birth
- Government-issued ID number (passport, national ID, or driver's license)
- A photo of your ID — front and back
- A selfie for facial matching

Verification usually takes a few minutes to 24 hours. Once verified, your KYC is universal — you never verify again when joining additional communities.

---

## PIN

Your PIN is a 4–6 digit code you create yourself during first login. It is never sent to you via SMS, email, or phone call — only you know it.

The same PIN works across all your communities and wallets. Avoid sequential numbers like 1234 or repeated digits like 0000.

- To change your PIN: **Menu → Security → Change PIN** (this affects all communities)
- If you forget your PIN: tap **"Forgot PIN"** on the login screen → receive an SMS reset link → create a new PIN

## Getting Started

1. Find an open community via their dedicated web portal, or receive an invitation from a community administrator
2. Download the HellOOpass Personal App from the **App Store** (iPhone) or **Google Play** (Android)
3. Open the app, enter your registered phone number and activation code
4. Complete KYC verification (once only — valid forever)
5. Create your 4–6 digit PIN
6. Your dedicated wallet is automatically created
7. You're ready to top up and start transacting!

## Joining a Community

To join a community, go to: **Discover → Community** → then select the community you want to join.

There are 3 types of communities:

1. **Open communities** — join instantly, no invitation needed. You can also join via the community's dedicated web portal.
2. **Request-based communities** — tap *Request to Join* after selecting the community. The admin will approve your request.
3. **Invitation-only** — you must first receive an invitation via SMS, email, QR code, or link from the administrator. Then go to **Discover → Community → Invitations** to accept.

Once you join, a dedicated wallet is automatically created for you — no new KYC or PIN needed.

## Wallets

Each community you join gives you one dedicated wallet. Wallets are community-specific — points from Community A cannot be used in Community B.

Each wallet:
- Stores Stablepoints backed 1:1 by real cash
- Accepts top-ups via multiple methods
- Lets you pay merchants in the same community
- Lets you send points to members of the same community

> **Note:** Cross-community transfers are coming at the end of June 2026.

---

## How to Top Up

There are 6 ways to top up your wallet:

1. **Cash to a Community Agent** — give cash to any community member acting as agent *(instant)*
2. **Bank Transfer** — via API integration *(instant)* or manual receipt upload *(up to 15 min)*
3. **Mobile Money** — MTN, Orange, Airtel, and others *(instant)*
4. **Receive points** from another member directly
5. **Merchant exchanges** your cash for points
6. **Sponsor top-up** via credit card portal *(for diaspora/NGO models)*

Tap **"Top Up"** on the dashboard to get started.

---

## How to Pay a Merchant

There are 5 ways to pay a merchant:

1. **Scan merchant QR code** — Tap Pay → scan → enter amount → PIN → Confirm
2. **Show your QR code** — select correct community first → Tap My QR → merchant scans
3. **Enter merchant's phone number** — Tap Pay → Enter Number
4. **OTP method** (for basic phones) — give your phone number, receive SMS OTP, read it to merchant *(valid 2 minutes)*
5. **Tap NFC card** near merchant's NFC reader

All app payments require your PIN. You receive a digital receipt after every payment.

---

## How to Send Points

1. Select the correct community
2. Tap **Send**
3. Enter recipient's phone number *(must be in the same community)*
4. Enter the amount in Stablepoints
5. Enter your PIN
6. Tap **Confirm**

> **Note:** You can only send points to members of the same community. Cross-community transfers are coming at the end of June 2026.

## Security Tips

- **Never share your PIN** with anyone — not even community administrators or merchants
- HellOOpass will **NEVER** ask for your PIN via SMS, email, or phone call
- Avoid simple PINs like 1234, 0000, or your birth year
- Change your PIN every 3 months via **Menu → Security → Change PIN**
- If your phone is **lost or stolen**, immediately contact your community administrator — they can block your wallets across all communities
- Then email **help@helloopass.com**

## NFC Cards

NFC cards are optional.

- **To activate:** Menu → NFC Card → Activate New Card → hold card to back of phone → set card PIN
- **To pay:** hold card near the merchant's NFC reader and enter card PIN if required
- **If lost or stolen:** Menu → NFC Card → Block Card → request a replacement from your community administrator

## Transaction History

To view your transaction history: tap **History** in the app.

You can filter by:
- Date range (today, this week, this month, custom)
- Type (payments, transfers, top-ups)
- Merchant or member name

To download a monthly statement: **History → Download → Select month** → choose PDF or CSV.

## No Smartphone / Basic Phone

If you don't have a smartphone, you can still use HellOOpass via the OTP method:

1. Tell the merchant your registered phone number
2. Merchant initiates the payment in their app
3. You receive an SMS with the merchant name, amount, and OTP code
4. Read the OTP to the merchant
5. Merchant enters it in their app
6. Payment is complete
7. You receive an SMS receipt

> OTP codes are valid for **2 minutes** only.

## Leaving a Community

**Menu → Communities → tap the community name → Leave Community → Confirm**

> **Important:** Your wallet balance must be zero before leaving (use, transfer, or withdraw all points first).

After leaving:
- Your dedicated wallet is closed
- Transaction history is archived (still viewable)
- You can re-join later (subject to community rules)
- Your KYC and other community wallets are unaffected

## Cross-Community Transfers

Cross-community transfers and purchases are **not yet available**. Points from one community can only be used within that same community.

This feature — allowing you to send points to members of other communities and pay merchants in other communities — is scheduled for release at the **end of June 2026**.

## Restricted Wallets

A restricted wallet is created by a sponsor with specific spending rules set in advance. Funds can only be used for approved purposes (e.g. health, education).

With a restricted wallet:
- You cannot send points to other members
- You can only pay approved merchants
- Spending categories and expiry dates may apply

This is used in the **Gift Card model (Model 4)** and **Disbursement model (Model 2)**. Most regular dedicated wallets are unrestricted.

---

## Withdrawals / Cash Out

Merchants and gig workers can convert their earned points back to real cash.

- **Merchants** must hold a valid official merchant license in their country to cash out
- **Gig workers**, as service providers, can also cash out their earnings
- **Regular end users and sponsors** cannot cash out directly — points are for purchasing within the community

If withdrawal is available to you: **Tap Withdraw → enter amount → select method → enter PIN → Confirm**

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| App crashes | Restart phone, clear app cache, or reinstall |
| QR not scanning | Clean camera lens, ensure good lighting, hold steady |
| "Insufficient balance" | Check balance, top up, or ensure correct community is selected |
| "Wallet does not belong to this community" | You selected the wrong community — switch to the merchant's community |
| Payment stuck on "Processing" | Check internet connection, wait 30 seconds, do not scan again |
| OTP not received | Check network signal, request resend, wait 1 minute |
| Forgot PIN | Tap "Forgot PIN" on login screen, receive SMS reset link |
| Cannot send to another community | Cross-community transfers coming end of June 2026 |
